Short description (max. 132 characters): The guardian for your AI prompts: checks for sensitive data locally before it reaches AI sites.

Description:

AImori is the guardian for your AI prompts. The extension checks what you type or paste into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot for sensitive data – before it is sent and right on your device.

What AImori does
• Pauses sending when sensitive data is found and explains why the value is sensitive.
• You decide: redact, use placeholders, edit or send anyway.
• Also warns about pasted screenshots (local text recognition), hidden instructions in texts and sensitive data in AI answers.

What is detected
• IBANs, card numbers, API keys, tokens and passwords
• Social security, tax and ID numbers of many countries, e.g. Swiss AHV number or German tax ID
• E-mail addresses, phone numbers, confidentiality markings and customer lists
• With the optional local AI also names, companies, addresses and health information
• Your own terms such as customer or project names, stored only as checksums

Privacy: everything stays on your device
• All checks run locally in the browser.
• No server, no account, no telemetry, no network access.
• Only settings and daily counters are stored – never your texts.

For organisations
• Central management with policies via GPO (ADMX, six languages) and Intune.
• Rules per data type, approved or blocked AI sites, separate rules for business and personal accounts.
• White label with your own logo, name and colours.
• Local report with numbers only on the measures taken (self-declaration, not legal advice).

Good to know
• Microsoft Edge does not let extensions run on copilot.microsoft.com: AImori cannot check input there. In Google Chrome, Copilot is protected.
• Not checked: AI side panels in the browser, AI extensions, desktop and mobile AI apps, and voice mode. AImori helps against mistakes and does not replace a DLP solution.

Languages: English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil).
Requirements: Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome version 110 or later.
